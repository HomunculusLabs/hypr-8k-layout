Centerstage trackpad and Kinesis hotkey controls

Hotkey 1: tap to arm ONE circular-motion gesture, then release the button.
  Move three fingers around a relaxed loop and lift them.
  Rough ovals, small gaps, contact wobble and extra same-direction laps are okay.
  It looks for looping intent, not geometric precision; even a boxy loop can count.
  The viewer's end gap is informational, not a requirement to return to the start.
  Clockwise/counterclockwise recognition only displays one notification.
  Normal three-finger navigation automatically resumes after the stroke.
  A second Hotkey 1 tap, Escape, context change, or 8-second timeout cancels.
  Cancelled/invalid strokes are consumed; they do not turn into navigation.

Hotkey 2: swap the focused window with the center-stage window, 1:1.
  When the swap finishes, focus lands on the window now in the center.
  Tapping again swaps straight back with the same partner (remembered per
  workspace); focus follows the window returning to center, so repeated
  taps ping-pong with you always typing into the center window.
  The previous-window action remains available via four-finger swipe down
  and Super+Tab.

The two physical buttons emit Ctrl+Shift+F11 / Ctrl+Shift+F12 internally. You do
not hold those keys yourself. The keyboard's current HID descriptor does not
support F13/F14. These assignments are saved in the keyboard's Base layer;
all other keys/layers were preserved. Hotkey 1 previously emitted Super+A;
Hotkey 2 was unassigned.

Three fingers left/right: focus the next populated left/center/right panel.
Three fingers up/down: previous/next window in the current panel, wrapping.
Four fingers left/right: native workspace swipe.
Four fingers down: previous managed window on the current workspace.
Four fingers up: open the searchable window picker; Escape closes it.
Super+Ctrl+G: toggle custom gestures and the one-shot shape layer. Native
workspace swipes stay enabled. Hotkey 2 remains available independently.

Swipe, then lift to commit. A long swipe still performs one action. Left/right
means physical finger direction, independent of application natural scrolling.
Both left subcolumns count as one panel. Re-entering a panel restores its last
focused window. Up/down follows stable slots/geometry, not recent-focus order.
Left/right stops at the outer edges. Two-finger scrolling and app zoom remain
unchanged. No shape action moves, resizes, retags, closes, or launches windows.

Focus/shape helpers are limited to managed Centerstage windows on workspaces
1-3. They are inactive in games, fullscreen, special workspaces, nondefault
keyboard submaps, and mapped interactive overlays. If Hotkey 1 says context is
unavailable, first focus a normal managed window. Shape classification remains
a prototype and needs calibration against real finger movements.

The old temporary Super+Alt shape adapter is no longer needed or loaded.
Runtime gesture toggles reset to enabled when config reloads. To disable
persistently, set enabled=false in gestures.lua.

Reload/check:
  hyprctl reload config-only
  hyprctl configerrors
Status, including the last shape result:
  hyprctl repl 'return require("hypr.gestures").status()'
Cancel all in-flight custom gestures:
  hyprctl eval 'require("hypr.gestures").cancel()'

Troubleshooting capture (off by default; one explicitly armed attempt only):
  hyprctl eval 'assert(require("hypr.gestures").capture_next_shape())'
  Then tap Hotkey 1 and draw the failing gesture once.
  hyprctl repl 'return require("hypr.gestures").shape_capture()'
  hyprctl eval 'require("hypr.gestures").clear_shape_capture()'
The capture holds at most 512 relative gesture points and rejection metrics in
memory. It never records ordinary scrolling, typing, screen locations or app
identity, and does not write a file. Further attempts do not overwrite it.
Reloading configuration or explicitly clearing removes it. Capture is refused
while a shape is already armed/in flight; finish/cancel that attempt first.

Recording a recognition corpus (several dozen strokes, per-stroke consent):
  cd /home/t3rpz/.config/hypr
  python3 scripts/shape-session.py --expect circle_cw --count 20
  python3 scripts/shape-session.py --expect circle_ccw --count 20
  python3 scripts/shape-session.py --expect negative --count 20
Each sample repeats the one-shot capture above; nothing is written unless you
answer keep. Files land in shape-corpus/ with a manifest.jsonl intent entry.
'negative' means three-finger strokes that must NOT trigger (swipes, zigzags,
figure-eights, scribbles), drawn through the same Hotkey 1 window.
Evaluate recognizer accuracy and inspect tuning metrics:
  python3 scripts/shape-eval.py
  python3 scripts/shape-eval.py --metrics turns
Delete shape-corpus/ to remove all recorded data; it is plain text files only.
