Centerstage trackpad controls

Three fingers left/right: focus the next populated left/center/right panel.
Three fingers up/down: previous/next window in the current panel, wrapping.
Four fingers left/right: native workspace swipe (unchanged).
Four fingers down: previous managed window on the current workspace.
Four fingers up: open the existing searchable window picker; Escape closes it.
Super + Ctrl + G: disable/enable custom Centerstage gestures. Workspace swipes stay on.

Swipe, then lift your fingers to commit. A long swipe still performs one action.
Left and right mean the direction your fingers move. These focus gestures are
not inverted by the natural-scrolling option used for application scrolling.

Both left subcolumns are one panel. Returning to a panel restores its last-used
window. Up/down uses stable layout order (right-panel slot numbers, otherwise
rows then columns), not recent-focus order. Left/right stops at the outer edges.

These gestures change focus only. They do not move, resize, retag, launch, or
close applications. The picker lists existing windows; selecting one may switch
workspaces explicitly. Two-finger scrolling/pinch and keyboard shortcuts remain
unchanged. Circular gestures and layout manipulation are not enabled.

Custom gestures do nothing outside Centerstage workspaces 1-3, on untagged/game
windows, in fullscreen, in special workspaces, in a keyboard submap, or while an
interactive layer such as the picker is mapped. Short/diagonal/cancelled swipes
and gestures whose focus/layout changes midway do nothing.

Runtime toggles reset to enabled on configuration reload. For a persistent off
setting, change enabled=true to enabled=false in gestures.lua. The four-finger
workspace binding remains independent of this setting.

Configuration:
  /home/t3rpz/.config/hypr/gestures.lua
  /home/t3rpz/.config/hypr/centerstage-navigation.lua
  /home/t3rpz/.config/hypr/centerstage-gesture-state.lua

Sensitivity is in gestures.lua: threshold=35, lock_distance=12, axis_ratio=1.3.
These are initial compositor-unit settings, not physical millimeters. Adjust
only after checking actual recognition and comfort. Reload after edits:
  hyprctl reload config-only
  hyprctl configerrors

Diagnostic mode (recognizes custom swipes but does not execute their actions):
  hyprctl eval 'require("hypr.gestures").set_diagnostic(true)'
Check the most recent custom gesture, without recording app titles or raw input:
  hyprctl repl 'return require("hypr.gestures").status()'
  hyprctl repl 'local e=require("hypr.gestures").last; return tostring(e.dx)..","..tostring(e.dy)'
Return to normal:
  hyprctl eval 'require("hypr.gestures").set_diagnostic(false)'

Isolated tests:
  cd /home/t3rpz/.config/hypr
  bash tests/run-centerstage-gesture-checks.sh

First hands-on check:
  Start in a center window. Swipe left, then up/down through the left panel.
  Swipe right back to center, then right to the right panel.
  Try four-finger down twice to switch out and back.
  Try four-finger up and dismiss the picker with Escape.
  Confirm two-finger scrolling/zoom and four-finger workspace swipes still work.
  Try Super+Ctrl+G to disable/re-enable the custom layer if anything feels off.
