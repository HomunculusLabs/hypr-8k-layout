DESKTOP AND WOW SETUP

Desktop
  Omarchy/Quickshell is retained; no second bar or notification daemon.
  Centered Everforest bar: 44px high, 12px bottom gap, larger text and icons.
  Simple date/time center, workspace controls left, system controls right.
  Notification cards: 440px at this theme's font scale, up to 3 body lines.
  Existing notification actions, history and DND remain in use.
  Super+N: show recent notification history.
  Super+Ctrl+N: toggle Do Not Disturb.
  Right-click a notification to dismiss it.

WoW (current Classic Beta installation)
  Launches floating on workspace 1, not forced fullscreen on workspace 4.
  Centerstage's normal initial viewport is 2560x1440 (16:9).
  Existing center windows move into the right sidebar when WoW takes center.
  Other Steam games retain their existing exclusions; Diablo rules unchanged.
  WoW stays opaque and skips blur, shadow, rounded-corner clipping, and resize
  animations. Fullscreen windows are excluded from automatic layout resizing.

Controls
  Super+P on WoW: put it in the center again.
  Super+F: native fullscreen; press again to restore the floating viewport.
  Super+R, then W: cycle center width; Escape leaves adjustment mode.
  Super+R, then T: cycle the available center height; Escape to leave.
  The game fits a 16:9 rectangle inside the selected area. Widening therefore
  grows both dimensions, subject to the available height.
